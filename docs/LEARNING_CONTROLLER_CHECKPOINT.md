## Model, assumptions and decision path

`memory-action-filter@5` fits one bounded exact canonical snapshot per foreground admission/evidence revision: at most 1,024 rows and 384 KiB. Indexed reads resolve sampled retractions outside the tail and inline decision references. Claims, exposure, passive presence, explicit inference and ordinary familiarity self-ratings do not become recall observations. Only declared, requested, unsupplied self-reported recall is admitted; task, assistance, item/version and directed-target distinctions survive. It is not independently scored performance. Missing observations are not failures.

The tractable particle filter couples task-conditioned recall to a separate prospective instruction transition. Action utility is signed integrated conditional capability-days relative to deferring to the next inferred opportunity or doing no extra work. It is not a weighted weakness/urgency score, Known fraction, measured causal gain, official score or pass probability. Early trajectory gains remain in the integral rather than optimizing only an assessment-time terminal value. Diagnosing knowledge and teaching capability receive separate value.

Consequential assumptions and safeguards:

- Initial memory: 27 equally weighted combinations of half-lives 0.5/7/60 days, possession 0.1/0.5/0.9 and three instruction kernels. Kernel growth is 1.1/2/4, acquisition 0.1/0.35/0.7 and failure refresh 0.5/1/2 days. These are unfitted engineering priors, not sourced mLearn treatment effects. Half-life extrapolation is capped at 3,650 days operationally.
- Reports: symmetric error 0.1 is an explicit assumption with alternative fits tested. Effect fitting requires at least 24 hours, compatible task and a different item/version. Recent revealed repetitions provide no independent observation or positive action extrapolation. Conditioning beliefs does not count as instructional benefit. Observational kernel fitting does not identify a causal advantage under policy selection.
- Outcome proxy: groups receive equal normalized weights; overlap uses maximum credit rather than duplication. These weights are not official score coefficients. Open use evaluates 30 days. A future deadline evaluates through the 90th percentile inferred opportunity gap afterwards, separately recording performance at the assessment point. That post-deadline horizon is not an early-readiness margin.
- Effort: task-conditioned lognormal active response time starts with median 30 seconds, log SD log(3) and two pseudo-observations, or a declared activity prior. Valid uninterrupted active timing updates it. Idle/background/stalled/interrupted wall time does not. Intervals use ±1.645 log SD with variance floor 0.01. Five equal-mass normal-quantile midpoint draws pair completion time with utility. Extreme tails and correlated interruptions remain unmodelled.
- Opportunities: latest 20 clean active bursts, separated by a 30-minute operational gap. Historical endings are censored rather than presumed voluntary. Sparse headroom draws are 60/120/300 seconds and gap draws 1/3/7 days; recent gaps and current absence replace the sparse renewal assumptions. Usage is not a calendar or exact free time. Preference and interruption-cost models are not fitted.
- Immersion: current intentional media requires an explicit target bridge. Home uses unfitted transfer 0.1 with 0/0.1/0.3 sensitivity and qualitative provenance. Unknown transfer remains unmodelled; passive presence is not comprehension. These are conditional comparisons, not a calibrated common efficacy scale across activities.

Physical action effects occur at completion. If a task cannot finish before an assessment, it receives no one-action assessment credit. Five effort/value draws govern feasibility together; the sequence solver compares total feasible benefit against continuing immersion, avoiding a gain/seconds race favoring trivial tasks. At most 32 candidates and disjoint one/two-action sequences are searched. Both execution orders compete: the second task completes after the first task effort, with the same trajectory end and deferred alternative. Positive improvement must exceed eight machine epsilons relative to compared utility to avoid selecting a zero-gain second task through roundoff; this is a numerical safeguard, not a learning-value threshold. Diagnostic-response timing remains an approximation.

Bounded information value compares future choices after either report against the same physical intervention with the report hidden. At most eight measured actions and eight alternatives compete, with at most the larger single information credit in a pair. Ordinary claims, passive immersion and recently revealed repetition receive none. This provides model-based useful exploration, not randomized causal identification or validated diagnostic efficacy.

Within one immutable calculation, wholly unobserved actions with identical family, transfer, effort, target weights and completion scenario reuse the exact prior computation, remapping addresses and trace. Fitted memories and journal state are never cached across decisions. Internal information branches omit unused performance forecasts. Exact-output regression checks guard this optimization. Ordered pair comparisons also reuse equivalent completion distributions and second-action physical functions only after eligibility/overlap checks. Identical prospective physical particle states sum probability mass; no fitted memories or canonical evidence are merged. Prepared scalar integrals preserve the full action calculation while avoiding repeated trace construction.

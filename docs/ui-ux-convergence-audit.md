## Remaining parallel implementations and limits

- `GrammarCoverage`'s construction list keeps three independent inline quality buttons per row. These are cue-visible self-reports on any of several constructions, with `translation: true` scaffold provenance when a meaning is shown. The live `RatingMatrix` pass is one blind, sequential written-form attempt with global keys, a durable cursor, and no meaning cue. Both use the canonical grammar journal writer and the same quality vocabulary, but the list interaction is semantically distinct from the matrix's single active recall attempt. A future focused-row design would be needed before giving list rows matrix keyboard ownership or Easy; the existing buttons are intentional.
- Review's pinned policy decision remains an optional, card-specific Inspector payload. Entry-kind Inspector targets remain distinct from surface targets.
- Flashcard Review and Browse both use `useFlashcardTts`, but retain different playback placement and timing. `WordHover.onPlayAudio` is an unused legacy prop worth discussing before removal. Word Sync's `T` reveals translation, not audio.
- Word/card shells, progress denominators, and navigation controls remain workflow-specific because they represent different prompts, queues, and destinations. Transient per-surface loading and failure states were verified through tests where they could not be held on screen manually.

## Post-convergence hardening

- All repeated surface Inspector callers now use `surfaceKnowledgeInspection(language, surface)`, which builds the same hashed graph target from Reader hover, sidebar, vocabulary, statistics, Word Sync, card review/browse, tutor setup, and character grid. Review adds its pinned trace as context; the helper does not invent one for other callers.
- A lightweight source contract checks that Review, Word Sync, Assessment, live Grammar, Home preview, and knowledge pill import and render `RatingMatrix`, and that their workflow boundaries still call the canonical attempt or grammar-journal writer. Existing component/integration tests exercise the submissions and persistence behavior. A helper test covers surface identity and optional trace context.
- Removed the unused `.flashcard-rating-btn`, `.flashcard-rating-label`, and `.flashcard-rating-time` rules. A search found no other safe-to-remove bespoke rating handlers or selectors. The remaining `policy-why__*` styles are live inside `PolicyTraceDetails` in Inspector.
- Playback smoke test: generated Japanese speech locally with macOS `say`, encoded non-silent Ogg Vorbis (Review card: 3.49 s, RMS 0.039) in the marked disposable profile, and clicked **Play word pronunciation** in both Browse and Review. The controls activated and no missing-audio or decode error appeared. Physical speaker output was not independently captured by a loopback recorder.

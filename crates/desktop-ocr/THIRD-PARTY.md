`src/detector.rs` adapts the contour/rotated-rectangle/score sequence from
Craun718/PaddleOCR-rs at `27fd3d77effa3f0032786a45d3566b64edcbe631`.
Preprocessing, thresholds, clipping, offsets and resource ownership are changed.
